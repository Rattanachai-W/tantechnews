# Weekly highlights carousel

Date: 2026-11-10

## Goal

Show a full-width weekly highlights carousel above the homepage hero.

## Rules

- Homepage page 1 only
- Pool: articles published in the last 7 days
- Pick 5 with a week-stable seeded shuffle (ISO week seed)
- UI: one large slide, prev/next, dots, slow autoplay paused on hover/focus and when `prefers-reduced-motion`
