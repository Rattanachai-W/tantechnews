# Advertise contact design

Date: 2026-11-10

## Goal

Let advertisers find how to contact TanTech News, without a form, pricing, or budget requirement.

## Decisions

- Placement: dedicated `/advertise/` page plus a short section on `/contact/`
- Channel: existing email `rattanachai.wp@gmail.com` with advertising subject line
- Depth: light intro, mailto CTA, checklist of what to include in the email
- Do not ask advertisers to state budget

## Email checklist (required copy)

- Brand / company name
- Preferred timing window
- Format of interest (banner, newsletter, sponsored, etc.)
- Sample link or short brief

## Discoverability

- Footer trust links: link to `/advertise/`
- Sitemap: include `advertise/` and `contact/`

## Out of scope

- Pricing table
- Ad inventory UI
- Separate ads inbox
- New emoji in UI copy
